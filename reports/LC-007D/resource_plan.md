# Proposed resource scope

**GPU_TRAINING_NOT_AUTHORIZED.** The present phase uses existing CPU environments and cached files only. The proposal covers one visible RTX 3090, six sequential fresh runs, 43,200 successful training updates, at most 5,400 charged seconds and a 2,147,483,648-byte process peak reserved cap. External downloads and disposable optimizer updates are both zero. LC-007B measured 4,043.21272 seconds for eight runs. A 6/8 scaling gives 3,032.4 seconds, about 50.5 minutes, as a rough historical estimate. Gates, validation load and concurrent training can change timing.

The deferred runner checks a dated owner record against Issue #23, its exact execution commit, the frozen configuration digest and all proposed limits. An operator must verify the record against the original chat message. The CPU preflight rechecks cached inputs and all eight historical references, then the authorized GPU scope snapshots GPU processes and verifies available memory. Existing work continues with its original settings. Less than 2 GiB available, inability to collect the initial snapshot, or a mismatch in GPU model or BF16 support stops before training.

The accounting interval starts before the GPU preflight and extends through final synchronization and cleanup. It conservatively includes interphase CPU overhead as well as setup, transfer, training, live and EMA validation, serialization, and failed operations. The synthetic latency probe uses one warmup and five synchronized 512-pixel BF16 staged forwards for each gated arm and its matched ungated controls, with zero optimizer updates. Actual peak allocated and reserved bytes and elapsed phases are charged and retained. Before each training batch or validation image, the runner checks the remaining estimate and reserved memory alongside epoch-level synchronized phase checks. The allocator fraction and cooperative checks are process-based controls.

Stop on predicted budget overrun, OOM, nonfinite states, skipped or missing successful updates, a checksum mismatch, storage or synchronization error. Save existing outputs, phase ledger and queue status. This queue has no automatic rerun, resume, alternate hyperparameters or extra seed. Hosted CI, official test, NWPU/WiderPerson and Stage 2 are outside this scope. [Issue #23 resource proposal](https://github.com/xinnan369/looped-transform/issues/23)
