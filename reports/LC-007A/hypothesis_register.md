The observed B loss–MAE divergence occurs with a common cosine schedule and affects recurrent and CNN controls. A paired EMA readout changes no loss, LR, horizon or data selection. Changing the schedule or horizon would require additional trajectories and would confound treatment with update exposure. LC-004A already found a mixed frozen-BN response in one A seed. Keep BN policy fixed in the screening proposal. LC-004B did not support count-cutoff calibration as a standalone explanation. No stabilization effect is estimated here, and final weights or sparse snapshots cannot reconstruct the proposed update-by-update EMA.
