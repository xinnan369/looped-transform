# Confidence-guided cross-window refinement

S-guide uses one shared, ungated 192-wide, six-head block and four noncyclic window-8 calls with shifts 0/4/0/4. U-guide uses four independent blocks with identical operations. S-placebo uses the shared model with a permuted gain map. The common CNN, decoder, parameters and initial state are inherited from the corresponding LC-007G arm. S-guide and S-placebo have 1,596,614 parameters. U-guide has 2,931,206.

Use the existing call-2 point logits at stride 4 and detach them before the FP32 sigmoid. Compute `u = 4p(1-p)` per slot, take the maximum across the two slots, then max-pool aligned 2×2 groups with ceil handling to reach stride 8. Origin-aligned cells at stride 4 are valid when their origins lie inside the real tile or crop. Padding contributes zero before pooling, and only stride-8 cells with at least one valid stride-4 cell contribute to the sample mean. Derive validity from image geometry independently of head annotations or assignments.

For calls 3 and 4, compute `g = 1 + .25(u - mean(u))` once and reuse it for `x + g * (Block(x) - x)`. Gains are neutral on padded cells and lie within [.75, 1.25] on valid cells, with per-sample mean one up to FP32 arithmetic. The implementation uses FP32 residual arithmetic, casts to the block output dtype, and selects the original block output exactly where gain equals one. Every token runs through the same dense block and original attention schedule. No gradient flows through the gain map. Gradients through the recurrent states and the original two supervised reads remain active.

The placebo circularly shifts the row-major list of valid gains by `floor(N/2)` positions per sample before restoring it to the original grid. This preserves the exact gain multiset and excludes padding. The fixed synthetic check changes all 6 cells, with Pearson correlation about −0.74 against the aligned map. Real-data correlation is unmeasured. Both guidance modes use `Lfinal + .25 Lmid`, with exactly two decodes for ordinary training. Final-only deployment still decodes the midpoint once, and four-stage diagnostics reuse the same midpoint readout.

Dense cost at 512×512 is 55.677 G FLOPs for deployed or two-read inference and 84.744 G for the four-read validation path. LC-007G reports 41.144 G for its original final-only inference, so the candidate adds about 35.3% in dense FLOPs. Two FP32 residual interpolations add 4,718,592 add/subtract/multiply operations at 512×512, plus uncertainty-map operations, comparisons, casts and any placebo indexing. The audit profiler counts dense operations and the inherited channel-gate counters. GPU latency and peak memory remain unmeasured.

The map expresses model ambiguity near probability .5. A confidently wrong point can receive a small gain, and a missed head may have no uncertain slot nearby. A real-data association and the placebo comparison are required to assess this mechanism.
